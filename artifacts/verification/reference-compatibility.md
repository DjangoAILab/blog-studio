## Compatibility defect found and closed

One historical document uses `2022-07-014 23:25:51`. Hexo 6 normalizes that
legacy value to 2022-07-14, while the initial adapter rejected it. The adapter
now reproduces Hexo's three-digit-day normalization while rejecting impossible
calendar dates. The exact historical article now previews at its existing
`/2022/07/14/.../` path, and unit coverage preserves the behavior.
