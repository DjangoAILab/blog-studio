# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability. We value precise
technical disagreement, curiosity, consent, and care for the people whose data
and sites our software touches.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes openly.
- Avoid harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discrimination, doxxing,
  credential exposure, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainers or
GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
issue a warning, impose a temporary restriction, or permanently ban a
participant, proportionate to impact and history. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical, and retaliation is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project. It is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
