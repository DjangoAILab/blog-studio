# v0.2 Interaction Target Candidates

**Status:** owner selection required. **Generated:** 2026-08-04.

These candidates are visual decision artifacts, not production UI. They share
the same verified product model and realistic Chinese content, while varying
the spatial system, materials, density, and motion model. Production surfaces
must not be visually rewritten until the owner records one selection in
[`v0.2-design-selection.md`](../verification/v0.2-design-selection.md).

## Shared contract

Every candidate covers the same four journeys on desktop and mobile:

1. first run discovers a Site, confirms “王二的博客”, and establishes owner
   credentials without showing a deployment token;
2. one content library shows `已发布`, `草稿`, and `已发布 · 有修改` together;
3. article editing is paired with an always-available Markdown full preview;
4. `准备更改` opens a three-file ChangeSet review and makes clear that a local
   commit does not publish or deploy the Site.

All candidates retain stable opaque paper for content, Markdown, and diffs.
Transparency is limited to navigation, command bars, and transient controls.
They exclude statistics, AI, Agent, and speculative future controls.

## Candidate 1 — 墨室叠页 / Ink Room Layers

A warm editorial room with a persistent Site rail, ivory paper, ink typography,
moss state color, and restrained cinnabar actions. Navigation and toolbars form
thin translucent layers above stable content. Motion should feel like lifting,
placing, and collecting sheets; mobile collapses the rail into the top journey
selector and keeps the primary preparation action visible.

This is the closest continuation of the existing paper-and-ink identity and
therefore has the lowest visual migration risk.

## Candidate 2 — 光井工作室 / Lightwell Studio

A brighter, more spatial workspace with a compact vertical Site spine, generous
breathing room, moss primary actions, and a floating command dock. Content
surfaces open from their own edge rather than from a fixed dashboard grid.
Mobile becomes a quiet single-column writing surface with a bottom command
sheet.

This direction most strongly expresses the desired light, fluid interaction
quality, but it needs the strictest density testing on large libraries.

## Candidate 3 — 出版轨道 / Publishing Rail

A deep-ink frame surrounds stable ivory content. The journey is explicitly
numbered from Site setup through content, editing, and ChangeSet review, making
local commit and later remote release feel like distinct gates. Motion should
advance or retreat along that rail rather than dissolve between generic pages.

This direction provides the strongest operational confidence and audit
narrative, at the cost of a more technical tone.
