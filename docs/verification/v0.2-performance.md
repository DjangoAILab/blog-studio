# v0.2 Performance Verification

Verified on 2026-08-06 against the production Studio build on the owner
workstation. The fixture is local and disposable: it registers one Git-backed
Site, creates 92 drafts plus one published document, and never contacts a
Provider or production content tree.

## Budgets

- Unified-content list requests: p95 at or below 100 ms with at least 90
  documents.
- Acknowledged autosave requests: p95 at or below 100 ms.
- Navigation animation frames: p95 at or below 20 ms and no frame above 50 ms
  while moving across `内容 / 系统 / 站点`.
- No build or generator-preview time is included in these interaction budgets.

## Reproducible measurement

With the normal Studio E2E fixture server running:

```sh
corepack pnpm --filter @blog-studio/studio exec playwright test \
  ../../artifacts/verification/v0.2-performance.mjs
```

The retained verifier performs 50 content-list samples, 30 sequential
acknowledged autosaves, and 271 animation-frame samples. It asserts every
budget and exits non-zero on a regression.

## Result

| Measure               | Samples | Median |    p95 | Maximum |
| --------------------- | ------: | -----: | -----: | ------: |
| Unified-content list  |      50 | 5.3 ms | 7.9 ms | 21.2 ms |
| Acknowledged autosave |      30 | 1.3 ms | 2.5 ms |  2.9 ms |
| Navigation frame      |     271 | 8.3 ms | 9.0 ms |  9.3 ms |

Navigation produced zero frames above 50 ms. All four automated gates passed.
These loopback measurements verify the product interaction budget; they do not
claim public-network latency or generator build performance.

The final authenticated home-server journey also recorded, but does not use as
the loopback gate, 50 HTTPS list samples at p95 187.0 ms and 30 acknowledged
autosaves at p95 230.5 ms. That measurement includes workstation-to-home-server
TLS/network latency; its temporary working copy was discarded and the final
content library reported zero modified documents.
